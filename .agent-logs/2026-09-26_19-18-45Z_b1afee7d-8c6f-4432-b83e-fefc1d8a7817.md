The issue you're seeing in the screenshot is a classic CSS Flexbox circular dependency bug. 

**What went wrong:**
The `InsightsPanel` had responsive percentage-width classes (`lg:w-[40%] xl:w-[35%]`), but it was wrapped inside a generic `<div className="h-full z-0 relative">` Right Column container in `MeetingClient.tsx`. 

Because that Right Column wrapper was a flex item without any width defined, it tried to dynamically shrink-wrap its contents. But since its child (`InsightsPanel`) was trying to take 40% of its parent's width, the layout engine got confused and collapsed the panel down to its `min-w-[400px]` fallback. This left the remaining allocated space empty, showing the dark `bg-slate-900` body background on the far right.

**The Fix:**
I've surgically moved those responsive width constraints (`w-full lg:w-[40%] xl:w-[35%] 2xl:w-[30%] min-w-[400px] shrink-0`) directly onto the Right Column wrapper in `MeetingClient.tsx`. 

Now, the Right Column strictly commands exactly 40% (or 35%/30%) of the total screen real estate, and the Left Column (`flex-1`) automatically and perfectly expands to fill the remaining 60%. The dark void is gone!
